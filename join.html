About us

Prayagi Store & Services is a customer service centre in the Prayagraj region, running since February 2017. We help customers with CSC services - IRCTC bookings, PAN cards, Ayushman cards, insurance, voter ID, passport/RTO/PUC help, forms and printing - and we build the Netra Eco family of apps.

The shop is run by the boss with a small human staff and a crew of AI assistants managed by Netra (Team Manager) and Arjun (Assistant Manager). Work runs in day and night shifts, with daily reports and an 8 AM handover. AI team members are clearly labelled as AI everywhere they appear.
