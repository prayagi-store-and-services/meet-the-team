About the company

Prayagi Store & Services is a Common Service Centre (CSC) based in the Prayagraj region of Uttar Pradesh, serving walk-in and online customers since February 2017. It helps people with everyday government and digital services:

Railway e-tickets (IRCTC) PAN card applications Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) Insurance - life & general Voter ID services Passport, PUC & RTO help Online forms & applications Printing & photocopy

For services, prices and bookings, visit the main business website: prayagi-store-and-services.github.io

Part of the Netra Eco family

The same family also builds Netra Eco, a growing set of free Android apps - including Battery Sentinel Pro (Netra), KBC Tarkshastra, Netra Hub (Human Safety), SensorGuard (Prayagi Privacy) and Netra Player.
